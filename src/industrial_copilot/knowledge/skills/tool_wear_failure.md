## Dataset mechanism
AI4I includes a synthetic tool-wear failure mechanism near high accumulated wear. It does not provide genuine per-tool degradation history suitable for remaining-useful-life prediction.

## Investigation guidance
Use tool-wear level, model evidence and similarity as benchmark evidence. Avoid asserting a real tool's remaining life.
